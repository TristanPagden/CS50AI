# Analysis

## Layer 7, Head 12

Nouns seem to pay close attention to their determiners. As you can see both dog and cat pay attention to the determiner "the".

Example Sentences:

- The cat [MASK] on the mat
- The dog [MASK] on the log

## Layer 1, Head 5

Pronouns seem to pay close attention to the verbs following them. As you can see both he and she pay attention to the verb "runs".

Example Sentences:

- He runs [MASK] the store
- She runs [MASK] the park
